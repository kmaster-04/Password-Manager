# Participant Consent Form
## Password Manager Usability Study

**Purpose:** This study evaluates the usability of a local password manager developed as a student capstone project. You will be asked to perform a series of tasks using the tool.

**Duration:** Approximately 15–20 minutes.

**What you will do:**
- Follow a one-page quick-start guide to create and use a password vault
- Complete 5 tasks while an observer records completion time and any errors
- Complete a short usability questionnaire at the end

**Data collected:**
- Task completion times and success/failure
- Any errors you encounter
- Your questionnaire responses (anonymised)

**Confidentiality:**
- No real credentials will be used; you will only enter synthetic data
- Your identity is not recorded; results are anonymised
- Data is used solely for this academic project

**Voluntary participation:**
You may stop at any time without penalty.
